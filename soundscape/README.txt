SOUNDSCAPE FILES for the POLH 2.5D world
These live in the parkour-game repo on GitHub, at parkour-game/soundscape/...
Any file can be replaced later by uploading one with exactly the same name. A missing file is simply silent.
BEFORE UPLOADING: this folder still has old generated placeholders for splash.mp3, portal_shoot.mp3 and
wind.mp3 - don't upload those over your own. Only jets / land / land_bobot / step here are still needed.

WHAT EACH FILE DOES
  wind.mp3              the sky above the city at normal volume, 50% louder in the wind that blows you back,
                        and muffled (with everything else) while you're in the water
  portal_shoot.mp3      the moment the portal leaves P4RK-0UR UNIT's hand
  portal_open_loop.mp3  loops while any portal is open - his, and the one that opens and closes at the top
  splash.mp3            jumping/falling into the water
  jets.mp3              Bobot's foot jets (loops while they fire)                       [generated, included]
  land.mp3              P4RK-0UR UNIT landing after a drop                              [generated, included]
  land_bobot.mp3        Bobot landing after a drop                                      [generated, included]
  step.mp3              general footstep, until step_forest / step_city are added      [generated, included]
  step_forest.mp3       ONE footstep on dirt - each step plays at a very slightly different pitch, faster when sprinting
  step_city.mp3         ONE footstep on the footpath - played quietly and subtly
  forest.mp3, city.mp3  ambience loops
  peckpeck.mp3          a magpie touching the player

NO LONGER USED (safe to delete from GitHub): underwater.mp3, portal_suck.mp3, portal_pop.mp3

To hear new sounds in Unity's Play mode or "Test in browser" before they go to GitHub, drop them into
the newest "POLH 2.5D upload vNN\GitHub parkour-game\soundscape" folder.
